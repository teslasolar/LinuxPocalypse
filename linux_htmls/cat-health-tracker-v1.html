⚖️ Weight Guidelines

  • Underweight: Ribs easily visible, minimal fat
  • Ideal: Ribs felt but not seen, waist visible from above
  • Overweight: Ribs hard to feel, no waist definition
  • Obese: Fat deposits visible, distended abdomen

💡 Healthy Weight Tips

• Feed measured portions, not free-feeding
• Use puzzle feeders for mental stimulation
• Aim for 15-20 min play sessions twice daily
• Consult vet before any diet changes

⚠️ Emergency Signs - See Vet Immediately

• Difficulty breathing • Collapse or unconsciousness
• Severe bleeding • Unable to urinate (esp. male cats)
• Ingested poison/toxin • Seizures
• Severe pain • Pale or blue gums

🍽️ Nutrition & Diet ▼

Essential Nutrients:

  • Protein: Cats are obligate carnivores - need meat-based protein
  • Taurine: Essential amino acid only found in animal tissue
  • Fatty Acids: Omega-3 and Omega-6 for coat and skin health
  • Water: Cats often don't drink enough - consider wet food

🚫 Toxic Foods

Onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes, raisins, alcohol, caffeine, xylitol, raw eggs, raw fish

🏥 Common Health Issues ▼

Dental Disease: Affects 70% of cats over 3 years. Signs: bad breath, drooling, difficulty eating.

Kidney Disease: Common in older cats. Signs: increased thirst/urination, weight loss, vomiting.

Hyperthyroidism: Overactive thyroid. Signs: weight loss despite good appetite, hyperactivity, vomiting.

Diabetes: Signs: increased thirst/urination, weight loss, weakness in hind legs.

Gallbladder Issues: Signs: jaundice (yellow skin/eyes), vomiting, lethargy, loss of appetite. Can be caused by infections, stones, or inflammation.

UTI/Bladder Issues: Signs: straining to urinate, blood in urine, crying in litter box. EMERGENCY in male cats if blocked.

😺 Behavior & Body Language ▼

Tail Position:

  • ⬆️ Up: Happy, confident, friendly greeting
  • ❓ Question mark: Playful, curious
  • ⬇️ Down/tucked: Fearful, anxious
  • 🌀 Puffed up: Frightened, trying to look bigger

Ear Position:

  • Forward: Alert, interested
  • Sideways/back: Irritated, anxious
  • Flat back: Fearful or aggressive

Purring: Usually contentment, but can also indicate pain or stress (self-soothing)

🏠 Environmental Enrichment ▼

The 5 Pillars of Cat Welfare:

  1. Safe place to hide and rest
  2. Multiple separated resources (food, water, litter, scratching)
  3. Opportunity for play and predatory behavior
  4. Positive human interaction
  5. Environment that respects their sense of smell

💡 Enrichment Ideas

• Vertical space (cat trees, shelves)
• Window perches for "cat TV"
• Puzzle feeders
• Rotating toys
• Catnip, silver vine, or valerian

🧓 Senior Cat Care (7+ years) ▼

Age-Related Changes:

  • Decreased activity and mobility
  • Changes in sleep patterns
  • Weight changes (loss or gain)
  • Cognitive changes (confusion, vocalization)
  • Dental issues more common

Senior Cat Needs:

  • Twice-yearly vet checkups
  • Easy-access litter boxes (low sides)
  • Steps/ramps to favorite spots
  • Warmer sleeping areas
  • More gentle handling
