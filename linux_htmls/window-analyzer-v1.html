🔍 Signs of Window Problems

Foggy Glass: Condensation between panes means seal failure. The insulating gas has escaped, reducing energy efficiency by up to 50%.

💨 Draft Detection

Hold a lit candle near window edges on a windy day. Flickering indicates air leaks. Common at corners, meeting rails, and weatherstripping.

🪵 Wood Rot Signs

Soft spots, peeling paint, discoloration, or visible fungi indicate rot. Press with screwdriver - healthy wood resists, rotted wood gives way.

🌡️ Energy Efficiency Ratings

U-Factor: Lower is better (0.20-0.40 typical). SHGC: Solar Heat Gain - lower blocks more heat. VT: Visible Transmittance - higher = more light.

📏 How to Measure for Replacement

Measure width at top, middle, bottom (use smallest). Measure height at left, center, right (use smallest). Measure depth of frame for proper fit.

💰 When to Repair vs Replace

Repair: Single pane cracks, weatherstripping, hardware, minor rot. Replace: Seal failure, extensive rot, warped frames, single-pane to double-pane upgrade.
